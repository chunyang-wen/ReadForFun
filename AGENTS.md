## Media
- All media files like image, audio files, please put them in cloudflare R2 bucket.
- Do not add unnecessary files to the repository. Commit only what a page needs to display: source, text, and data.
- Research drafts, process images, screenshots, PDFs, and local media caches stay out of Git. Publish display images and audio to R2 instead.
